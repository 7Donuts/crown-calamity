# Quay Outfitter and Gear Resale

The Quay Outfitter buys gear once per owned item ID. Starter alternatives are in stock at level 1: rod and sword (18 coins), precision blade (20), buckler (12), focus (15), charm (16), and workcoat (20). Head, hands, and feet options are available early: ledger cap (22), dockhand gloves (16), and canal-walking boots (16). Sluicewalker boots require level 2 and cost 24. These choices are reachable after a few ordinary hunts, and a new character with 20 coins can buy a weapon or a defensive piece immediately.

The familiar level-1 items keep their original combat values and identifiers. Shop purchases are unique; an owned item cannot be bought a second time. Buying returns a clean catalog copy, never upgrades or affixes left from a previous sale.

An owned item can be sold only while unequipped and outside a fight or delve. Sale removes its bag ID and all per-item workbench modifications in the same mutation. The loadout is never altered by a sale. The resale value starts at 25% of the item’s base outfitter value, rounded down, plus at most 3 coins for upgrade ranks; affixes add no resale value. A 45% base-value ceiling keeps every sale below the original purchase price. Items without a listed shop price use a modest level-based appraisal for resale only. Crafting and upgrades cost more than the coins they can return, so buying, crafting, upgrading, and selling cannot create an infinite coin loop.

Fight and delve locks apply to buying and selling. Each preview reports its price, resulting item, and a reason when the action is unavailable. There are no stock resets or passive coin gains. All coins enter play through explicit rewards and are spent or recovered through explicit actions.
