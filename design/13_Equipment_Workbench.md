# Equipment and Workbench

Equipment is a set of nine named slots: weapon, offhand, head, body, hands, feet, accessory, accessory 2, and relic. Items remain in the unique-ID bag while equipped. The two accessory slots accept the same accessory catalog; one owned ID can occupy only one slot. Equipping moves that ID to its new slot. A two-handed weapon occupies the weapon slot and leaves offhand empty and unavailable until a one-handed weapon is equipped. A weapon cannot be removed while it is the only weapon, because every character must retain the universal Attack action.

The original item IDs and their established traits remain stable for save compatibility. New alternatives fill head, feet, and relic slots at levels 1–6, with a level-7 breach pike providing a clear two-handed tradeoff. Attribute and defense bonuses stay modest and legible. The Copper-Thread Charm remains a level-1 Luck purchase at 25 coins.

Items can be bought or exchanged once. A duplicate loot roll continues to salvage into one Salvaged fitting in the combat reward path. Existing guaranteed fight materials remain the crafting inputs: Ledger scraps, Canal iron, Runic residue, Chainback shell, Stamped brass, Floodglass, Floodglass Fragment, Salvaged fittings, and Repair fittings. Early recipes convert low-tier drops into fittings or repair materials, and can assemble the level-1 cap, level-2 boots, and level-3 gate shield. Later recipes turn Runic residue into Floodglass and Floodglass into fittings. The guaranteed Floodglass Fragment from every boss clear can be exchanged for three Floodglass and two Repair fittings; five fragments can instead forge the level-8 Seal or Band, provided the selected reward is not already owned.

Safe equipment changes are unavailable during fights and delves. Every purchase, exchange, upgrade, and affix application checks the lock, ownership, level, and full coin/material cost before spending anything. Upgrade levels are item-specific and capped at three; each level adds one point to the item's primary base, armor, ward, spell base, or primary attribute (two base for the two-handed pike). Costs rise from 35 to 120 coins and consume the item family’s existing materials. Modified names include their prefix, suffix, and upgrade rank.

The workbench offers optional deterministic prefixes and suffixes. An owned item may have one of each. Affixes are slot-compatible flat bonuses, have explicit coin and material costs, and replace an existing affix of the same kind. Affix coin costs use the upgrade chapter multipliers (1×, 4×, 16×, 64×, and 256× across Chapters 1–5), while material costs remain unchanged. Applying the same affix twice is rejected without spending. There are no random rolls or hidden success chances. Item upgrades and affixes persist in `itemMods` by base item ID.

State migration retains known equipped items and valid per-item modifications, removes duplicate slot occupancy, and recognizes all nine slots. Old version-2 saves remain valid; an equipped legacy item missing from the bag is restored to the bag during normalization.
