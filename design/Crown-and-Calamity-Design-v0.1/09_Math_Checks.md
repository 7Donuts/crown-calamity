# Preliminary arithmetic checks

Checked against the v0.1 candidate formulas. These are calculation and document-consistency checks, not a playtest or proof of balance.

| Example | Result |
|---|---:|
| Sera E10 invested attribute total | 96 |
| E10 HP / stamina / mana | 612 / 130 / 142 |
| Physical accuracy vs evasion 55 | 57 vs 55 |
| Resulting hit chance | 85.39% |
| Rank-2 Driving Blow raw damage | 107.19 |
| Mitigation against armor 70 | 28% |
| Noncritical HP damage, no barrier | 77 |
| Same attack against Guard | 50 |
| Rank-2 Mend | 84 HP |
| Rank-2 Aegis | 70 barrier |
| Total XP E1 to E10 | 3,972 |
| Proposed ordinary overall rare drop chance | 1% |
| Five repeated boss clears with no 20% rare roll | 32.77% |
| Selected boss rare via fragment exchange | 5 eligible clears maximum |

The rare-roll drought is why the proposed selected-item fragment exchange exists. Five clears do not guarantee a random rare drop; they guarantee enough fragments for the selected exchange, if the player saves them.

Also checked: hit chance bounds across extreme sampled rating pairs, mitigation bounds across sampled defenses, continuous effective level at four ascension transitions, and local Markdown link targets.

The agreed revision uses one exchange per tap with mobile-first controls. The single-action arithmetic above is unchanged. These checks do not validate combined Attack/Cast actions, their cost, interface comfort or overall balance; those remain prototype tasks.

Not checked: complete build win rates, encounter time, defensive stat dominance, boss damage tuning, expected campaign leveling time, ability rotation efficiency, bot detection quality or player enjoyment. Those remain prototype tasks.
