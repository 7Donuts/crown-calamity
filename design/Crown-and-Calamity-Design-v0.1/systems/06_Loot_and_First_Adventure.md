# Loot and the first playable adventure

Status: small content proposal for testing the solo experience. Names and story are placeholders. A full economy, crafting profession, world atlas and bestiary are deferred.

## One city: Bracken Quay

A crowded canal town sits above a public flood-control dungeon. Its streets remain dry because an ancient magical authority renews the flood barriers. The authority has begun treating residents as unauthorized obstructions. Someone must enter the works before the next river surge.

Only five city destinations are needed: the contract board, equipment vendor, recovery house, archive/trainer and canal gate. Each is a panel with a person, purpose and immediate action, not an empty explorable scene. The character's home and biography are accessible from the sheet.

Two initial races are enough to prove character creation: Human and Stonekin. Both start with the same 60-point budget and can learn every ability. Humans begin with Prepared; Stonekin begin with Sure Footing. These passives occupy the same slot and have the same attunement cost. The other passive becomes learnable during the introductory chapter. Race also changes several lines of dialogue and one background hook. The long-term racial-trait design remains open.

Three suggested practice kits: sword/shield, blade/charm and rod/focus. They are equivalent starter packages with free early swapping, not classes. A starter shop exchange lets the player try the other kit without grinding.

## The first chapter: Below the Waterline

1. **Drainage Inspection:** A maintenance worker is trapped by Ledger Rats. Select a practice kit and tap through a first ordinary fight using familiar actions; expand its report to inspect the effects. Rescue the worker and receive the first level's remaining XP from a once-only contract reward.
2. **A Permit with Teeth:** Investigate a stolen access seal. Learn to read an intent and use a second action. Select coins or repair materials; the decision does not conceal a permanent power advantage.
3. **The Bent Gate:** Face a heavy winding attack. A brief trainer lesson unlocks Disrupt, with a non-interrupt route using Guard and recovery.
4. **A Job for Someone Expendable:** Choose whether to recover a missing crew ledger or secure a failing pump. Each leads to a different room and story contact, with equivalent reward budgets.
5. **The Municipal Works:** Enter the solo delve, use its limited camp and defeat the Acting Superintendent. Finish the chapter with evidence that the system's original purpose has been distorted.

The worker survives if rescued; the choice appears in the journal and later dialogue. The story needs one consequential callback before it needs a branching campaign framework.

## Six regular enemies and one boss

These are encounter briefs and initial HP targets, not finished stat blocks. All versions resolve one exchange per tap. Ordinary versions should allow comfortable action repetition; their patterns explain build performance and can be inspected in the report. Harder versions reward changing actions at useful moments. Assign full ratings and rewards after testing the combat worksheet.

| Enemy | E / draft HP | Pattern | Decision to teach |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ledger Rat Swarm | 1 / 140 | Two nibbles, then a visible rush | Guard against burst rather than every turn |
| Canal Scavenger | 2 / 170 | A guarded stance alternates with an exposed attack | Save the costly hit for an opening |
| Permit Wisp | 3 / 210 | Announces a ward, then casts a bolt | Choose a different damage channel or conserve resources |
| Chainback Crab | 5 / 280 | A slow clamp can be disrupted; high armor | Interrupt or use arcane damage |
| Siltbound Inspector | 7 / 360 | Marks a hazard; next round rewards Brace or Guard | Follow a two-round consequence |
| Floodgate Hound | 9 / 440 | Tracks repeated attacks, then adopts a defense against that channel | Change approach without making one build unusable |
| Acting Superintendent | 10 / 620 | Three-phase pump guardian with visible pressure changes | Combine learned responses and resource planning |

The hound's adaptation is limited to 15% additional mitigation for two rounds and triggers only after three consecutive damaging actions of one channel. Guard or Recover breaks the sequence. A pure physical build still has a response and cannot become permanently hard-countered.

## The Municipal Works: short solo delve

This is marked focused content, entered voluntarily. Normal leveling does not require repeatedly completing it.

Five nodes: gatehouse → safe inspection point → choice of records room or overflow chamber → one camp → pump chamber boss. No real-time movement. Each node shows current resources, likely danger and the consequence of withdrawal.

The records room rewards investigation materials and backstory; the overflow chamber rewards equipment materials and a harder combat. Both paths reach the boss. The camp has one recovery charge for the run. Returning to town ends the run, retains completed room rewards and starts a fresh run on re-entry. No camp charge farming between persistent boss attempts.

Boss phases use explicit text labels:

- **Inspection:** Alternates basic pressure and a visible heavy stamp. Guard or Disrupt handles the stamp.
- **Pressure Release, below 65% HP:** Opens a valve objective. Spend an action to vent, or accept a known stronger attack and continue damage. Venting needs no particular stat.
- **Emergency Procedure, below 30% HP:** Reveals alternating armor and ward openings. Both channels can finish; using the opening improves efficiency. Every third round is a stronger telegraphed attack, preventing free indefinite recovery.

Damage targets should leave a reasonable low-defense build able to survive one unguarded heavy attack. A major telegraph should be threatening but should not kill a fresh, appropriately equipped character from full HP. Exact enemy output is a required prototype task.

## Small equipment model

Initial slots: main hand, offhand, head, body, hands, feet, two accessories and one relic. If nine slots create too much early inventory work, unlock accessory/relic use after the first dungeon rather than filling them with meaningless starter items.

Each item needs only an ID, name, optional illustration, slot, E requirement, weapon/focus base if relevant, armor/ward values, up to two attribute bonuses and optionally one authored trait. Detailed affixes, sockets, sets, durability grades and random crafting outcomes can wait.

| Example | Role in the first slice |
|---|---|
| Canal-Iron Sword | E1 heavy weapon, base 14; establishes a readable baseline |
| Surveyor's Blade | E1 precision weapon, base 14; scales with Finesse instead of Might |
| Inspector's Rod | E1 arcane focus, base 14; can use Basic Strike with spell power while equipped |
| Riveted Gate Shield | E3 offhand, +18 armor; competes with a focus/charm |
| Chalkbound Focus | E3 offhand, +3 spell item base; offers output instead of shield armor |
| Oilskin Coat | E3 body, +12 armor and +8 ward; modest mixed defense |
| Chainback Grips | E5 hands, +2 Might and +6 armor |
| Lantern-Eye Charm | E6 accessory, +2 Insight; comparison demonstrates accuracy/evasion effects |
| Superintendent's Seal | E8 relic; once per encounter, first useful Guard grants 15 barrier |
| Floodglass Band | E8 accessory; +2 Intellect, +8 ward; alternative to a trait item |

The rod's Basic Strike uses its own item base and spell power; it does not also gain the offhand base a second time. Illustrations never substitute for numbers or text requirements. As content scales, item bases must be deliberately budgeted; an E10 sword with base 30 is the worked arithmetic example.

## Rewards and targeted progress

Every successful eligible encounter grants its listed XP, a small coin amount and a known material. Ordinary hunts remain fully eligible. Changing actions more often does not itself multiply rewards or improve a loot roll. Optional equipment roll: 20% chance; conditional rarity split 75% common, 20% uncommon, 5% rare. Overall rare probability is therefore 1%, not 5%. Display this in an inspectable loot table.

First boss completion guarantees a choice of two equivalent uncommon items, including one behavior-changing option. Subsequent eligible boss clears have a proposed 20% chance of a rare item and always award one Floodglass Fragment. Exchange five fragments for a selected item from the boss's rare pool. The deterministic route matters more than a spectacularly low drop chance.

Dungeon items may offer interesting alternative interactions, but normal hunt/vendor/material gear must support comfortable leveling. Do not balance the next ordinary hunting band around owning an optional boss drop. This keeps deeper play attractive without making tactical farming compulsory.

First-clear bonus is keyed to the character/account progression record, not a client flag. Resetting a run cannot reissue it. Excess inventory goes to a claim chest with no automatic deletion in the prototype. Vendor starter gear remains available so bad luck cannot block progress.

## Tiny economy, for now

Use coins for vendor equipment, affordable repairs and later respecs; materials for one selected-item exchange. Reward values are provisional and must be measured against actual encounter time. The prototype needs no player market, auction house, tradable premium currency or supply-chain simulator.

A gear upgrade should create one of three readable changes: better output, better survival, or a new interaction. If it only changes an imperceptible decimal, combine it into a more meaningful reward or remove it.

## The reward administrator

Working name: B.U.R.S.A.R., the Bureau of Unreasonably Rigorous Salvage Assessment and Remuneration. It began as an emergency allocation system and now insists that heroic deeds need correct documentation.

It speaks after important results, not every click. Examples of original achievement text:

- **Load-Bearing Citizen:** Guard against three actual heavy attacks. “You have discovered the tactical applications of remaining upright. Engineering has been informed.” Reward: title and a repair bundle, once.
- **Percussive Maintenance:** Successfully interrupt the Superintendent's stamp. “The approved shutdown procedure was unavailable. Apparently you were available.” Reward: 20 coins, once.
- **Not a Clerical Error:** Rescue the canal worker and return their ledger. “One citizen restored to the correct column. Please stop making that sound heroic.” Reward: a journal entry and cosmetic seal.
- **Budget Emergency:** Finish the delve without spending its camp charge. “Resources conserved. Therapist not included.” Reward: cosmetic banner, once; no permanent stat bonus.

Achievements use explicit event conditions and server-issued grants. The narrator's apparent intelligence does not require a live language model or authority to invent mechanical rewards. Authored text is enough to test the feeling.
