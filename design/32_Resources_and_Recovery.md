# Resources and Recovery

Resources should create tactical choices during a fight and a light recovery decision between fights. At level 1 with the original 68-point bulwark allocation, maximum mana is 66 and maximum stamina is 65. The formulas are `floor(30 + 2 × Will + Intellect + racial bonus)` mana and `floor(35 + 1.5 × Vitality + 1.5 × Finesse + racial bonus)` stamina. All current/max resources stay integers, including with fractional relic echo bonuses. Gear and later attribute investment continue to grow both reserves. Raising a maximum through allocation, gear changes, or upgrades never restores current health, mana, or stamina; current resources are only capped if a maximum falls. Respeccing and reallocating therefore cannot be used as free healing.

The free Attack and Defend actions remain available at any resource level. Defend restores 2 stamina after the enemy response. Against a declared heavy attack Defend prevents 65% instead of 35% and grants Opening for the next exchange (design 14); Opening restores nothing and never affects resources, and Sidestep, like other zero-mana actions, regenerates 2 mana. Every accepted combat action that spends no mana restores 2 mana, capped at maximum mana. This includes Attack, Defend, physical skills, Recover, Vent and Withdraw, and applies to the final blow as well. A mana-spending spell grants no regeneration. Regeneration happens once after the player's action, before encounter completion or the enemy response; it is never granted by an invalid action, a rejected rapid tap, reading state, waiting, navigating or loading a save. Stamina has no general passive regeneration. Recover restores its base 8 mana plus the 2-mana regeneration, for up to 10 mana total, and 10 stamina. It uses the player's exchange and still allows the enemy response. The combat log and action feedback show actual mana regenerated; resource caps can reduce it to 1 or 0. There are no idle or offline gains. Prepared grants 3 mana and 3 stamina when a fight begins.

Past the story (effective level above 50) a victory restores 60 Bounce (stamina), capped at maximum Bounce (D21, `POST_STORY_VICTORY_BOUNCE` and `postStoryVictoryBounce` in `dist/growth.js`; G3-5). It resolves inside the final exchange, after the Cocoa heal and before the victory receipt, so it needs no input and no timer; it is shown in the victory log ("Victory restored 60 Bounce (stamina).") and the spoils note, and recorded as `bounceRestored` on the victory receipt only when something was restored. A defeat, a withdrawal, a rejected action and an exchange that does not win restore nothing, and through effective level 50 a victory restores no Bounce, as before. The amount is flat because Bounce costs are flat: a fight spends about 65 to 70 Bounce (Driving Strike 10, Guardstrike 12), the real kits' maximum is 177 to 252 for the physical Breaker at Realness 1 and 2 and over 1,000 at Realness 9, and a share of the pool would refill a Realness 9 pool several times over while leaving a Realness 1 Breaker short. Measured in G3-5 (30-fight unrested chains), before the restore a Breaker with a planned pair drained its Bounce by about fight 5 and then spent 18% to 45% of its exchanges on the free Attack (and 8% to 17% on a plain Guard with no strike); after it, no Breaker or Duelist configuration spends more than 5% of its exchanges on plain Attacks. Past the story a victory also restores 45 Sparkle (mana), capped at maximum Sparkle (D23, `POST_STORY_VICTORY_SPARKLE` and `postStoryVictorySparkle` in `dist/growth.js`; G3-6), by the same rules as the Bounce restore: resolved inside the final exchange right after it, no input and no timer, shown in the victory log ("Victory restored 45 Sparkle (mana).") and the spoils note, recorded as `sparkleRestored` on the victory receipt only when something was restored, and nothing for a defeat, a withdrawal, a rejected action or an exchange that does not win. Through effective level 50 a victory restores no Sparkle, as before. The amount is flat for the same reason as Bounce: Arc Bolt and Wardcast cost a flat 16 Sparkle (12 with the economy mastery), a fight spends about 80 to 90, and Sparkle pools run from about 100 for a Breaker to about 280 for a Realness 1 Caster and 1,700 for a Realness 9 Caster; a share of the pool would refill a big pool many times over. **How 45 was fitted** (G3-6, 30-fight unrested chains, 40 seeds, the worst of the eight Caster configurations at E60, the smallest Sparkle pool; the bar is 5% of exchanges on the free Attack): in G3-5 a Caster with a planned pair ran short of Sparkle after about five fights and spent up to 27% of its exchanges on the free Attack; with a restore of 0 the worst configuration is still 33.3%, at 30 it is 8.6%, at 35 4.4%, at 40 1.5%, at 45 0.6%, at 50 0.1% and at 55 and 60 0%; at E100 and above every value from 35 up is 0%. 45 is the first multiple of 5 that puts the worst configuration under 1% (a margin of 4.4 points under the bar; 35 passes by only 0.6 points). It is not free: a fight costs 80 to 90 Sparkle and a win returns 45, so a Caster that keeps winning still loses about 40 Sparkle a fight and has to Recover (Shake it off restores 10 Sparkle) or answer a heavy with Hunker down, which costs no Sparkle, every few fights.

Arc Bolt costs 16 mana, or 12 with its economy mastery. Wardcast costs the same mana plus 10 stamina. Mend costs 24 mana. Driving Strike costs 10 stamina; Brace 12; Disrupt 16; Guardstrike 12; Sidestep 12; Aegis 14; Expose 10. These prices make repeated expensive actions impossible with a starter reserve while leaving free basic actions and Recover available.

Rest is explicit. `beginRest(state, now)` records a ready timestamp four seconds later; it does not heal or schedule an action. A player must call `finishRest(state, now)` after that timestamp to restore health, mana, and stamina. An early claim fails without changing state. `cancelRest` clears the pending timestamp and grants no recovery. Rest is unavailable during a fight or delve, while already resting, or at full resources. Starting a fight is blocked while a rest is pending, even after its timestamp, until it is explicitly finished or cancelled. This uses the same client clock model as the existing action delay; a future authoritative server must own these timestamps.

Defeat removes 5% of carried coins, rounded down and capped at 50 coins. Zero coins remain zero. There is no experience or equipment loss. A defeat also records a 12-second `woundedUntil` timestamp. The defeat receipt reports the actual `coinsLost` and `recoveryReadyAt`. Starting another fight is blocked until the wound expires. A rest begun during the wound shares its deadline: its ready time is the later of four seconds from beginning and the wound timestamp, rather than stacking two waits. Saves preserve both timestamps; loading a save does not advance or complete recovery. Full-heal helpers used by authored content and fixtures can clear a pending rest, but they retain the wound timestamp so they cannot bypass the death lock.
