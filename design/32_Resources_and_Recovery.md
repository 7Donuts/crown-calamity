# Resources and Recovery

Resources should create tactical choices during a fight and a light recovery decision between fights. At level 1 with the original 68-point bulwark allocation, maximum mana is 66 and maximum stamina is 65. The formulas are `floor(30 + 2 × Will + Intellect + racial bonus)` mana and `floor(35 + 1.5 × Vitality + 1.5 × Finesse + racial bonus)` stamina. All current/max resources stay integers, including with fractional relic echo bonuses. Gear and later attribute investment continue to grow both reserves. Raising a maximum through allocation, gear changes, or upgrades never restores current health, mana, or stamina; current resources are only capped if a maximum falls. Respeccing and reallocating therefore cannot be used as free healing.

The free Attack and Defend actions remain available at any resource level. Defend restores 2 stamina after the enemy response. A completed exchange gives no automatic mana or stamina, and there are no idle or offline gains. Recover restores 8 mana and 10 stamina, uses the player's exchange, and still allows the enemy response. Prepared grants 3 mana and 3 stamina when a fight begins.

Arc Bolt costs 16 mana, or 12 with its economy mastery. Wardcast costs the same mana plus 10 stamina. Mend costs 24 mana. Driving Strike costs 10 stamina; Brace 12; Disrupt 16; Guardstrike 12; Aegis 14; Expose 10. These prices make repeated expensive actions impossible with a starter reserve while leaving free basic actions and Recover available.

Rest is explicit. `beginRest(state, now)` records a ready timestamp eight seconds later; it does not heal or schedule an action. A player must call `finishRest(state, now)` after that timestamp to restore health, mana, and stamina. An early claim fails without changing state. `cancelRest` clears the pending timestamp and grants no recovery. Rest is unavailable during a fight or delve, while already resting, or at full resources. Starting a fight is blocked while a rest is pending, even after its timestamp, until it is explicitly finished or cancelled. This uses the same client clock model as the existing action delay; a future authoritative server must own these timestamps.

Defeat removes 5% of carried coins, rounded down and capped at 50 coins. Zero coins remain zero. There is no experience or equipment loss. A defeat also records a 12-second `woundedUntil` timestamp. The defeat receipt reports the actual `coinsLost` and `recoveryReadyAt`. Starting another fight is blocked until the wound expires. A rest begun during the wound shares its deadline: its ready time is the later of eight seconds from beginning and the wound timestamp, rather than stacking two waits. Saves preserve both timestamps; loading a save does not advance or complete recovery. Full-heal helpers used by authored content and fixtures can clear a pending rest, but they retain the wound timestamp so they cannot bypass the death lock.
